Help us help you

You will get a better answer, faster, if you tell us a few things.

  • Your dates in Krabi or Khao Lak
  • How many of you are coming, and the ages of any children
  • Where you are staying
  • What you enjoy: beaches, snorkelling, paddling, something on land
  • Anything we should know, such as a non-swimmer in the group

Already booked?

If your question is about an existing booking, include your booking reference and tour date. For changes to your pickup, message us on WhatsApp as early as you can.

Private or custom tours

Our scheduled tours are shared trips. If you have something private or custom in mind, email contact@lovenaturekrabi.com with your dates and group size, and we will tell you what is possible.

Frequently asked questions

About getting in touch.

What is the fastest way to contact you?

WhatsApp is the fastest way, on +66 96 407 5194. You can also email contact@lovenaturekrabi.com.

What are your opening hours?

Our office in Ao Nang is open every day from 7am to 10pm. Online enquiries are answered 24/7, and we usually reply to messages within two hours.

Can I ask for advice before I book?

Yes, and we encourage it. Tell us your dates and who is travelling, and we will suggest the tour we would pick.

How do I book a private tour?

Email us at contact@lovenaturekrabi.com. Our scheduled tours are shared, and we will tell you what private options are possible for your dates.

Do I need to visit your office to book?

No, you book online and we collect you from your hotel.
