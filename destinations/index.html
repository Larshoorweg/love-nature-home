Places to visit in Krabi, chosen by locals

Ten places we know well, with plain advice on what each one is like and who it suits.

Where to go in Krabi

Three kinds of day out

There are more places to visit in Krabi than most people can fit into one holiday, so it helps to know how they differ. Some are islands you reach by boat. Some are on the mainland and need no sea crossing at all. Two of them are not in Krabi but further north, in Phang Nga province.

Pick a place below to read what to expect, how to get there and when to go. Where we run a tour, the page links to it.

Islands off the Krabi coast

Longtail and speedboat days
014 IslandsThe classic first day out, with four stops close to Ao Nang and a sandbar you can walk at low tide. 02Hong IslandsA lagoon inside the cliffs, a curved beach and a viewpoint climb, a little further up the coast. 03Koh Phi PhiThe famous pair of islands, including Maya Bay. A longer crossing and a busier day.

On the Krabi mainland

Reached by road, no boat needed
04Ao Thalane MangroveA kayaking area of mangrove channels, lagoons and limestone canyons. 05Klang CaveTham Khlang in Ao Luek, a cave you explore with a guide, then a swim at the Blue Lagoon. 06Tiger Cave TempleA working monastery near Krabi Town with a 1,260 step climb to the summit. 07Railay BeachPart of the mainland, but cut off by cliffs, so you arrive by boat. 08Klong MuangA quiet beach area north of Ao Nang, and a good place to stay if you want calm evenings.

Further north, in Phang Nga

A little past Krabi, worth the ride
09Khao LakA mainland beach town, and the starting point for our Surin Islands trip. 10Surin IslandsA national park far out in the Andaman Sea, open for part of the year, with snorkelling and a Moken village.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

About choosing between places.

Which places in Krabi are good for snorkelling?

The 4 Islands and the Hong Islands both include snorkelling stops close to Krabi. For a full day built around snorkelling, the Surin Islands trip from Khao Lak has three stops.

Which places can I visit without a sea crossing?

Ao Thalane, Klang Cave, Tiger Cave Temple and Klong Muang are all on the mainland. Ao Thalane is still a water day, because you explore it by kayak.

Are all of these places in Krabi province?

No, eight of them are. Khao Lak and the Surin Islands are in Phang Nga province, north of Krabi, and our Surin trip picks up from Khao Lak hotels.

When is the best time to visit Krabi's islands?

November to April is the dry season, with the calmest sea. Boat trips still run from May to October, but they change or cancel more often because of weather.

Do you run tours to every place on this page?

Nearly all of them. We run tours to the 4 Islands, Hong Islands, Koh Phi Phi, Ao Thalane, Klang Cave, Tiger Cave Temple and Surin Islands. Railay is a stop on our 4 Islands trip, and Klong Muang and Khao Lak are places to stay.
