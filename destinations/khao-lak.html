Get to know Khao Lak

Where our Surin trip begins

Khao Lak is a beach area on the mainland of Phang Nga province, north of Krabi. For us, Khao Lak tours mean one thing: it is where our Surin Islands snorkelling trip begins.

We are a Krabi company, so we will be straight with you. This page is here to explain how Khao Lak fits into a trip with us. It is most useful if you are staying in Khao Lak, or planning a few nights there as part of a longer route.

Our tours here

Our tour from Khao Lak

We run one trip from here: a full day to the Surin Islands, with three snorkelling stops and a visit to a Moken village. The day includes a buffet lunch, snorkelling gear, the national park entrance fee and insurance.

Where it is

North along the Andaman coast

Khao Lak is in Phang Nga province, on the Andaman coast north of Phuket and north-west of Krabi. It is a different province from Krabi, and it is a road journey away, not a boat ride.

By road, Khao Lak is about 140 km from Ao Nang, and the drive takes two to three hours. You can book this transfer with our partner, Paradise Sea Travel and Tours.

Worth knowing

Can I do the Surin trip from Krabi?

Not as a day trip with us. Pickup for this tour is from Khao Lak hotels only, and the pickup is early in the morning.

If you are based in Krabi and want to see the Surin Islands, plan at least one night in Khao Lak before the trip. If you only have days in Krabi, the Hong Islands or the 4 Islands are the better fit.

Best time to visit

When to come

The Surin Islands are a national park that closes for the monsoon. This season the park reopened on 15 October 2026 and is due to stay open until mid-May 2027. The exact dates are set each year by the park authority, and our trip runs only while the park is open.

Within the season, the sea is usually calmest from November to April.

Tips before you go

Pack light, pack right

Book your Khao Lak hotel before you book the trip, so we know where to collect you.
Go to bed early. The day starts with a morning pickup and a long boat ride.
If you get seasick, take your tablets before the crossing.
Bring swimwear, a towel, sunscreen and a dry bag.
Good to know

Frequently asked questions

About Khao Lak.

Is Khao Lak in Krabi?

No, Khao Lak is in Phang Nga province, north of Krabi. You travel between the two by road.

What tours do you run from Khao Lak?

We run one tour from Khao Lak, a full day snorkelling trip to the Surin Islands with a Moken village visit.

Do you pick up from Krabi or Ao Nang for the Surin Islands trip?

No, pickup for this trip is from hotels in the Khao Lak area only. Guests from Krabi usually stay in Khao Lak the night before.

When does the Surin Islands season run?

The park is open for roughly seven months of the year. This season it reopened on 15 October 2026 and is due to stay open until mid-May 2027.

Is the national park fee included on this trip?

Yes, on our Surin Islands trip the park entrance fee is included.
