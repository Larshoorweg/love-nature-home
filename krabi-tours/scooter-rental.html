Description

Explore Krabi your way with a hotel-delivered motorbike rental—convenient, affordable, and perfect for independent travelers. Ride along scenic roads, stop when you like, and soak in the freedom.

Read more

Scooter rental is one of the easiest and most enjoyable ways to see Krabi. Whether you're chasing sunset views at Klong Muang Beach or cruising through the backroads to a hidden viewpoint, having your own two wheels makes it all possible. We deliver your scooter straight to your hotel in Ao Nang, so you can start your adventure without the hassle of going to a rental shop.

Our scooters are well-maintained, reliable, and easy to ride. You’ll get two high-quality helmets included, so you're ready to go right away. Plan your route or just follow your curiosity—ride to local markets, stop for lunch at a roadside stall, or explore quiet coastal roads at your own pace.

This 24-hour rental gives you total flexibility, whether you want a sunrise ride or a late evening cruise. You choose the start time, and we’ll handle the rest.

Important Information

What to bring

  • Valid driver’s license (international license recommended)
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen
  • Water bottle
  • Lightweight backpack for your things

Dress code: Casual and comfortable. Wear closed shoes or sandals with a good grip, especially if you plan to explore off the beaten path.

Health & Safety: Always wear your helmet while riding. Stick to the left side of the road and follow local traffic rules. Avoid riding in heavy rain or unfamiliar areas at night.

Accessibility: Rentals are available to riders aged 18 and up with a valid license. Some steep or remote roads may not be suitable for beginners.

Weather: Krabi is warm year-round, but sudden rain showers are common. Plan ahead and avoid slippery roads during heavy rain.

Environmental Responsibility: Ride respectfully through local villages and natural areas. Avoid littering and help preserve Krabi’s stunning environment by staying on marked roads.

Pickup Details: Scooter delivery is available from 9 AM to 8 PM. Just send your preferred time via WhatsApp to +66 096 407 5194. We'll deliver right to your hotel in Ao Nang.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Still wondering about something? Just send us a quick message. We’re happy to help.

Contact Us

1. Do I need a motorbike license to rent a scooter in Krabi?

Yes, you need a valid motorbike license from your home country. An international driving permit (IDP) with a motorcycle endorsement is strongly recommended for insurance coverage and legal compliance.

2. What happens if the motorbike breaks down?

If you're within the Ao Nang area, we offer basic support to assist you. For safety reasons, we recommend staying on paved roads and within the local area.

3. Can I choose the delivery and pick-up time?

Yes! You can pick any delivery time between 9 AM to 8 PM. Your 24-hour rental period starts from the delivery time you choose.

4. Do I have to return the motorbike with a full tank?

Yes please refill as you return the motorbike. We deliver it with a full tank and please return it the same way.

5. Can two people ride one motorbike?

Yes, all motorbikes come with two helmets and are suitable for two passengers. Just ensure both riders are comfortable with the weight and handling.
