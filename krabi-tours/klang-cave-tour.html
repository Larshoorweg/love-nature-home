Description

Get ready for a unique half-day adventure through Krabi’s hidden gems. This guided tour takes you deep into the mysterious Tham Klang Cave, followed by a refreshing swim and kayak at the scenic Blue Lagoon (Klong Sa Kaeo).

Read more

Tham Klang Cave is one of Krabi’s lesser-known wonders. Tucked away from the crowds, this limestone cave is filled with striking formations, cool chambers, and stories shaped by time. With a knowledgeable guide by your side, you’ll learn about its geology, hidden corners, and natural beauty.

After the cave, unwind at the peaceful Blue Lagoon. Locally called Klong Sa Kaeo, this spot is famous for its crystal-clear water and tranquil jungle setting. Whether you swim, paddle a kayak, or simply soak up the scenery, it’s a refreshing contrast to the cave’s darkness. It's a short, enriching trip perfect for nature lovers who want to see a wilder side of Krabi without spending the whole day.

Itinerary

  1. 9:00 AM – Hotel Pickup

    Start your day with a convenient pickup from your hotel in Ao Nang. Please be ready in the lobby at least 10 minutes before your pickup time.

  2. Drive to Tham Klang Cave

    Enjoy a scenic ride through quiet countryside roads as you make your way to the entrance of Tham Klang Cave with your local guide.

  3. Explore Tham Klang Cave

    Spend 1 to 1.5 hours exploring the cool, shaded chambers of this limestone cave while learning about its unique rock formations and local legends.

  4. Arrive at the Blue Lagoon

    Reach Klong Sa Kaeo, known as the Blue Lagoon, a peaceful freshwater spot surrounded by lush forest and famous for its crystal-clear water.

  5. Swim and Kayak

    Cool off with a swim or paddle around the lagoon. Kayaks, safety gear, and basic instruction are provided for a fun, relaxing experience.

  6. Seasonal Fruit Snack

    Take a short break to enjoy local seasonal fruits and cold drinking water, perfect for recharging after your swim and exploration.

  7. 1:30 PM – Return to Hotel

    Wrap up your adventure with a comfortable ride back to your hotel in Ao Nang, arriving by early afternoon with great memories and photos.

Important Information

What to Bring:

  • Swimwear and towel
  • Lightweight hiking shoes or trainers
  • Sunscreen and insect repellent
  • Hat or cap for sun protection
  • Waterproof bag for your belongings

Dress Code: Wear light, breathable clothing suitable for walking and water activities. Bring a change of clothes if you plan to swim or kayak.

Health & Safety: This tour includes moderate physical activity like cave walking and kayaking. Not recommended for pregnant guests or people with mobility or heart conditions.

Accessibility: The tour is not wheelchair accessible and not suitable for guests with limited mobility due to uneven terrain and steps inside the cave.

Weather: The tour operates rain or shine. In case of heavy weather, your guide may adjust the itinerary to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.

Environmental Responsibility: We follow Leave No Trace practices. Please avoid touching cave formations and dispose of any waste responsibly to help protect this natural area.

Pickup Details: Pickup is available from hotels in the Ao Nang area. Your guide will meet you at your hotel lobby at 9:00 AM. Please be ready 10 minutes before pickup time.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Still wondering about something? Just send us a quick message. We’re happy to help.

Contact Us

1. Is this tour suitable for kids?

This tour is only suitable for children aged 10 and above due to cave walking and water activities. It’s best for kids who are comfortable swimming and walking on uneven ground.

2. Do I need to know how to kayak?

No prior kayaking experience is needed. Your guide will give a simple briefing, and the lagoon is calm and easy to paddle.

3. What happens if it rains?

The tour runs rain or shine, and light rain often makes the experience more adventurous. In case of storms, the itinerary may be adjusted for safety.

4. Can I join if I can’t swim?

Yes, but swimming and kayaking are optional. Life jackets are provided, and you can relax by the lagoon if you prefer not to go in the water.

5. How long is the drive from Ao Nang?

The drive takes around 30–40 minutes each way, depending on traffic. It’s a scenic route through the countryside.
