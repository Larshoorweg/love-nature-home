About Love Nature Krabi

A local tour operator in Krabi, and a friend on your journey

We are Love Nature Krabi Travel and Tour, a locally owned tour operator based in Ao Nang, Krabi. Krabi is home for us. We would like to show you around the way we would show a friend. Since 2022 we have been taking guests out to the islands, the mangroves and the jungle.

Our story

It started at a hotel desk on Koh Phi Phi

Long before Love Nature Krabi was a local tour operator in Krabi with a team of ten, Supaporn Sueakaew sat at a reservations desk in a hotel on Koh Phi Phi. Day after day she watched travellers arrive, head out to the islands and come back again.

It made her curious. She is from Krabi, and she found she wanted to know these places properly, not just book the rooms beside them. So she began to study them seriously: where people went, and what made a day out a good one.

One idea stayed with her: "Good tourism activities have to come from local people." Someone local knows what the weather is about to do, which trip is worth your day, and which guide will make it. In 2022 she turned that idea into Love Nature Krabi.

"The best friend on your journey."

Our plans

Looking after Krabi

We put "Love Nature" in our name on purpose. The sea, the mangroves and the islands are the reason any of us have this work, and we want the generations after us to have the chance to see them too.

So our plan is to build the company around conservation-focused tourism. We are at the start of that, and we will not pretend otherwise. As the plans become real, we will share them here.
