Which tour suits you?

Not every tour fits every traveller. Here is what we tell friends when they ask.

Travelling with young children4 Islands by longtailShort boat hops, shallow water and plenty of beach time. Good to know: the beaches get busy around midday.
You want quiet more than famousHong IslandsFewer boats, a calm lagoon and a viewpoint. Good to know: the lagoon depends on the tide, so timing matters.
Not a confident swimmerAo Thalane kayakingYou stay in the kayak the whole way, and a guide can paddle for you. Good to know: you will get splashed.
You came for the big namesKoh Phi PhiMaya Bay and the classic views. Good to know: it is a long day on the water and you will share it with many others.
The sea looks rough, or it is rainy seasonKlang Cave and Blue LagoonA land day that works in any weather. Good to know: some walking and steps inside the cave.
The beach is not enough actionATV rideMud, jungle tracks and a guide in front. Good to know: wear clothes you do not mind getting dirty.

Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most before a booking.

All FAQs
How do I book a tour?

Pick a tour and a date on the booking page and pay in full online through Stripe's secure checkout. You will get a confirmation straight away, and we message you with your pickup time. Prefer to talk first? Send us a WhatsApp.

Is hotel pickup included?

Yes, on most tours. We pick up across the Krabi and Ao Nang area: Krabi Town, Ao Nang, Ao Nam Mao, Klong Muang and Tubkaek. Staying somewhere else? Tell us where and we will sort out a meeting point.

What happens if the weather is bad?

We check the sea every morning, and a tour can change with the weather. You can cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before the start time. After that, bookings are non-refundable.

Are national park fees included?

It depends on the tour. Each tour page lists what is included and what is not, so check the details before you book.

Can we book a private tour?

Yes. Tell us your dates, your group and what you would like to see, and we will suggest a day and a price.
