Welcome to Krabi

We are Love Nature Krabi Travel and Tour, a locally owned team that has been running Krabi tours since 2022. Our founder, Supaporn, is a Krabi local, and she leads a team of 10.

Krabi is limestone cliffs, warm shallow sea and mangrove forest, and there is more than one good way to see it. Our job is to help you pick the day that fits you, then look after you from hotel pickup to drop-off. We will tell you honestly what each trip is like, including the parts that are not for everyone.

Honest advice

Which tour suits you?

Here is how we would choose, if you asked us.

Still not sure? Message us and tell us who is travelling. We will say which one we would pick, and why.

First time in Krabi?4 Islands by longtailThe best-known trip here. Good to know: in high season you will share the beaches with other boats.
Want the most sea in one day?Hong Island and 4 Islands by speedboatBoth island groups in one day. Good to know: it is also our longest day out in Krabi.
Prefer paddling to beach time?Ao Thalane kayakingMangroves and lagoons, by kayak. Good to know: you paddle yourself, so come ready to use your arms.
Not keen on a sea crossing?ATV or Klang CaveThe ATV ride and the Tham Khlang Cave day both stay on the mainland. Good to know: the cave day ends with a swim at the Blue Lagoon.
Here for the snorkelling, with a spare day?Surin IslandsStarts in Khao Lak, with pickup from Khao Lak hotels. Good to know: the speedboat crossing takes about an hour and a half each way, so it is a full day.

Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most before a booking.

All FAQs
What Krabi tours do you run?

We run island trips to the 4 Islands, Hong Islands and Phi Phi, a 7 Islands sunset trip, kayaking at Ao Thalane, an ATV ride, a cave and Blue Lagoon day and a Tiger Cave Temple tour. We also run a Surin Islands snorkelling trip that starts in Khao Lak.

Is hotel pickup included?

Yes, pickup and drop-off are included on every tour. Krabi tours cover the Ao Nang and Krabi area, and the Surin Islands trip picks up from Khao Lak hotels. Check the tour page for the exact area.

How do I book and pay?

You book online and pay at checkout. Payments go through Stripe, and you get a confirmation by email.

Can I cancel my tour?

Yes, you can cancel up to 24 hours before the start time for a full refund. After that, the booking cannot be refunded.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If it is not safe to go, we cancel or move the trip. When we cancel, you can choose a full refund or a new date.

When is the best time to visit Krabi?

November to April is the dry season, with the calmest sea. From May to October there is more rain and the sea can be rough, so boat trips are more likely to change.

Do you offer private tours?

Our scheduled tours are shared trips. If you would like a private or custom day, email us at contact@lovenaturekrabi.com and we will tell you what is possible.
