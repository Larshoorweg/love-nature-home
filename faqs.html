Krabi tour FAQs

Booking and payment

How to book and pay.
How do I book a tour?

Choose your tour and date on our tours page and pay at checkout. You get a confirmation by email with your pickup details.

How do I pay?

You pay online when you book. Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

Can I pay in cash on the day?

No, tours are paid online at checkout. The only thing you may need cash for on the day is a national park fee, on the tours where it is not included.

Am I booking with Love Nature Krabi or with Jai Thai?

You are booking directly with us. Our booking pages run on the Jai Thai Travel booking engine, and we confirm your booking and run your tour.

Can I book at the last minute?

Yes, if there are places left. The calendar on each tour page shows what is still open, and you can message us on WhatsApp if you are unsure.

Cancellations and weather

Changing plans, and what the sea decides.
What is your cancellation policy?

You can cancel up to 24 hours before the start time for a full refund. Cancellations after that cannot be refunded.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If it is not safe to go, we cancel or move the trip. When we cancel, you choose between a full refund and a new date.

Can I change the date of my tour?

Yes, you can change your tour date free of charge up to 24 hours before the start time, the same as for cancellations.

Do tours run in the rainy season?

Yes, most of our Krabi tours run all year. From May to October boat trips change or cancel more often, and the Surin Islands trip stops while the national park is closed.

Hotel pickup

Where we collect you and when.
Is hotel pickup included?

Yes, pickup and drop-off are included on every tour.

Which areas do you pick up from?

For Krabi tours we cover the Ao Nang and Krabi area, including Krabi Town, Ao Nam Mao, Klong Muang and Tubkaek. The exact area depends on the tour and is shown on its page. The Surin Islands trip picks up from Khao Lak hotels.

What time will I be picked up?

The start time is shown on the tour page, and your pickup details come with your confirmation.

I am staying in Railay. Can I join?

Yes, but we cannot pick you up there, because Railay cannot be reached by car. Please join us at the meeting point, Nopparat Thara Pier. Plenty of boat taxis run from Railay to the pier.

On the tour

What is included and what to bring.
What is included in the price?

Every tour includes hotel pickup and drop-off, a licensed guide and insurance. Meals, equipment and other inclusions differ by tour, and each tour page has the full list.

Are national park fees included?

It depends on the tour. On some trips the park fee is paid in cash on the day, and on the Surin Islands trip it is included. Each tour page says which applies.

Is lunch included?

On some tours, yes. Several of our island trips include lunch, and the kayaking trip does not. Check the tour page before you book.

Can you cater for halal or vegetarian diets?

Yes. All the food we serve is halal. Vegetarian meals can be arranged on request, so tell us when you book or message us in advance and we will prepare one.

What should I bring?

For boat and kayak trips bring swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, a hat, sandals and a dry bag for your phone. For the cave, wear shoes with grip. Bring some cash on any tour.

How big are the groups?

Our tours are shared trips, and many of them take up to 12 guests. The group size is shown on each tour page.

What language do the guides speak?

Our guides speak English.

Children, swimming and fitness

Who can join, honestly.
Are your tours suitable for children?

Yes, most of them are, and families are very welcome. Each tour page lists its age categories, and we are happy to advise before you book.

Can I join a boat trip if I cannot swim?

Yes, you can enjoy the boat, the beaches and the views without swimming. Life jackets are provided, and you should tell your guide before any snorkelling stop.

How fit do I need to be?

Island trips are easy for most people. Kayaking means about two hours of paddling in a two-person kayak, and a guide can paddle for you for an extra fee. The cave visit is moderately active. The Hong Island viewpoint is a steep staircase and is optional. If you are unsure, ask us and we will be honest.

Can children join the ATV ride?

Yes, children can join as a passenger, seated behind an adult. There are no weight limits. Before you set off we talk the ride through with you and give a safety briefing on what to do and what not to do.

Safety and guides

Licence, insurance and our team.
Are you a licensed tour operator?

Yes, our tourism licence number is 33/10148, and our guides are licensed.

Is insurance included?

Yes, insurance is included on every tour. We still suggest having your own travel insurance for the rest of your holiday.

Who actually runs the tours?

Our own team does. Each activity has its own lead and guides, chosen by our founder, Supaporn.

Private and custom tours

For a private or custom day.
Do you offer private tours?

Our scheduled tours are shared trips. If you would like a private or custom day, email contact@lovenaturekrabi.com and we will tell you what is possible.
